Ambient Studio

Ambient Studio is the video production and publishing tool behind Revision 4 News (@revision4news) and Ambiance Escapism. It runs on its operator's own computer, where it makes ambient videos, short news videos with voice-over, and the Revision 4 News live stream, and publishes them to the operator's own accounts.

Ambient Studio is not a public service: nobody can sign up for it. It only connects to accounts that its operator owns or manages, and it only posts videos that its operator made with it.

What it does

  • Makes videos: ambient scenes with music, and short news explainers with an AI voice-over, captions and on-screen sources.
  • Publishes them to the operator's own channels on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram, with titles, descriptions and an AI-generated-content disclosure.
  • Shows the operator how those channels are doing: views, likes, followers and similar numbers from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X, Twitch and Kick.
  • Runs the Revision 4 News live stream, using the operator's own stream keys on Twitch and Kick.

Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Ambient Studio ("Ambient Studio", "we", "us") is the video production and publishing tool behind Revision 4 News and Ambiance Escapism. This policy explains what information Ambient Studio gets from the platforms it connects to, what it does with that information, where it is kept, and how to remove it.

In short: Ambient Studio runs on its operator's own computer and only connects to accounts the operator owns or manages. It reads those accounts' profile details and statistics, and posts videos the operator made to them. Everything it stores stays on that computer. We don't sell or rent any information, we don't show ads, and we don't track anyone.

1. Who this covers

Ambient Studio is not a public service and nobody can sign up for it. The only people whose accounts are connected to it are its operator and anyone the operator manages channels with. Those people choose to connect their accounts, and they can disconnect them at any time (see section 8).

Ambient Studio doesn't collect information about the people who watch, follow or comment on our videos. It only reads totals, such as view and follower counts.

2. What we get from each platform

Accounts are connected with each platform's own sign-in (OAuth), so Ambient Studio never sees a password. It asks only for the permissions listed here.

3. How we use it

  • To publish the videos the operator made to the accounts the operator chose, with the title, description, caption and visibility the operator set.
  • To show the operator how their channels and videos are doing, in Ambient Studio's own dashboards.
  • To send the Revision 4 News live stream to the operator's own Twitch and Kick channels.
  • To show which account is connected (its name and picture) and whether its sign-in still works.

We don't use this information for advertising, profiling or anything other than the uses above. We don't use information from YouTube, TikTok or Instagram to train AI models.

4. Where it's kept, and for how long

Ambient Studio stores everything on its operator's own computer: account details and statistics in a local database, and sign-in tokens in files that only the operator's user account can read. We don't run a server that receives this information, and nothing is uploaded anywhere except to the platform it belongs to.

  • Sign-in tokens are kept until the account is disconnected (or the platform revokes them), and are then deleted.
  • Account details and statistics are kept so the dashboards can show changes over time, until the account is disconnected or the operator deletes them.
  • Videos and their files are the operator's own work and stay on the operator's computer until the operator deletes them.

The operator's browser also keeps a few display preferences (such as which tab was open last) in its local storage. They contain no account information.

5. Sharing

We don't sell, rent or trade any information. Information from a platform is only ever sent back to that same platform, to publish a video or read statistics.

To make videos, Ambient Studio sends some content to AI services: for example the text of a news article and the script written from it, to write and fact-check the script and the video's title and description; and the script, to turn it into a voice-over. That content is the operator's own material and public news. No account information, tokens or statistics from the platforms above are sent to these services.

We would only disclose information if the law required it.

6. YouTube API Services

Ambient Studio uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel to Ambient Studio, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove Ambient Studio's access to your Google account at any time from your Google account's third-party connections page (also reachable at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions).

7. Other platforms' policies

Each platform's own policy covers what it does with your information: TikTok Privacy Policy, Instagram Privacy Policy, X Privacy Policy, Twitch Privacy Notice and Kick Privacy Policy.

8. Disconnecting an account and deleting its data

Any connected account can be disconnected at any time, which deletes its tokens, details and statistics from Ambient Studio. You can also remove Ambient Studio's access on the platform itself, or email us to have everything deleted. The data deletion page has step-by-step instructions for each platform. We act on deletion requests within 30 days and confirm by email.

9. Security

Ambient Studio talks to each platform over encrypted connections (HTTPS). It runs on its operator's computer, and the files holding sign-in tokens can only be read by the operator's user account. No system is perfectly secure, but we only keep what Ambient Studio needs, and only on that one computer.

10. Children

Ambient Studio is not meant for, and is not used by, anyone under 13 (or the minimum age for using the connected platforms where they live). We don't knowingly hold information about children.

11. Your choices and rights

If your account is connected to Ambient Studio, you can ask us what we hold about it, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing us. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA; we will honor them.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If a change affects how we use information from a connected account, we'll tell the people whose accounts are connected before it applies.

Data deletion

Effective November 24, 2026

Ambient Studio keeps a connected account's sign-in tokens, profile details and statistics on its operator's computer (see the Privacy Policy). Here's how to have all of it deleted. Any one of these ways is enough.

1. Disconnect it in Ambient Studio

In Ambient Studio, open Settings → Accounts, find the account, and remove it. This deletes its tokens, details and statistics straight away. For YouTube, TikTok, X, Twitch and Kick you can also choose to revoke Ambient Studio's access on the platform at the same time.

2. Remove Ambient Studio's access on the platform

Once access is removed, Ambient Studio can no longer read or post anything for the account. Then ask us to delete what was already stored (see 3).

  • Facebook / Instagram: on Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations, select Ambient Studio and click Remove. On Instagram, go to Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites, and remove Ambient Studio.
  • TikTok: in the TikTok app, go to Profile → ☰ → Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions (or Security → Manage app permissions), select AmbientStudio and remove access.
  • YouTube / Google: open your Google account's third-party connections, select Ambient Studio and remove all connections.
  • X: go to Settings → Security and account access → Apps and sessions → Connected apps, select Ambient Studio and revoke access.
  • Twitch: go to Settings → Connections, and disconnect Ambient Studio under Other connections.
  • Kick: go to Settings → Connections (or Connected apps), and remove Ambient Studio.

3. Email us

Email ambient.studio.contact@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and the platform and username of the account. We'll delete everything Ambient Studio holds about it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What isn't deleted

Disconnecting an account doesn't delete the videos already published to it; those are on the platform, and you can delete them there. Videos the operator made stay on the operator's computer as the operator's own work.
